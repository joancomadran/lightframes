What's changed in Lightframes, newest first. The full history is in the [commit log on GitHub](https://github.com/joancomadran/lightframes/commits/main).

## 20 October 2026

- **Range masks.** Local adjustments can now be limited to a brightness range (darkest to brightest) and to a colour range, with a smoothness control. Useful for touching only the sky in a linear mask, or only the warm tones inside a brush stroke.
- **Smoother panels on phones.** Swipe down on the editing panel to fold it away and up to bring it back.
- **Gear recommendations and donations.** A "Buy me a coffee" button, plus a small, labelled space with photography gear picks (affiliate links). See [About](/about) for how the project is funded.
- **Demo video.** The start screen has a button to watch a short video of the editor in action.

## 19 October 2026

- **Background removal.** Find the subject automatically, on your device, and replace the background with transparency, a solid colour or a blur. A refine brush (keep / erase) fixes the edges of the cutout.
- **AI upscaling on export.** Enlarge photos 2× or 4× when exporting, using a model that runs in your browser.
- **Teeth whitening.** A new "Whiten" slider for local adjustments.
- **Nine new built-in looks:** B&W, B&W Noir, B&W Matte, Sepia, Selenium, Teal & Orange, Warm Film, Bright & Airy and Nordic. Presets now show as a grid of thumbnails rendered from your own photo.
- **Choose what to paste.** Pasting edits opens a dialog where you pick which settings to apply, so you can paste the colour without the crop.
- **Multi-file downloads as a ZIP.** Exporting several photos or carousel slides now gives you one ZIP instead of a burst of downloads.
- **Faster session saving.** Large RAW files are stored only once in your browser's storage, so saving after each edit stays quick.
- **New sample photos,** developed from the author's own RAW files.
- **Clearer interface.** Icons on the panel tabs, a progress indicator in the toolbar for long tasks, an "Add photos" placeholder in the empty filmstrip and a tooltip explaining the clipping toggle.
- **New address.** Lightframes now lives at [lightframes.si](https://lightframes.si).

## 18 October 2026

- **Remove objects with AI.** In the Retouch panel, mark something you don't want and an on-device AI model fills the area in from its surroundings.
- **Spot removal.** Heal and clone spots in the Retouch panel, with a shortcut (H) to show or hide the spot outlines.
- **Collage and carousel mode.** Build Instagram collages and seamless carousels that continue across slides, with ready-made layouts and a choice of slide shapes.
- **Built-in presets** to start from, and a before/after view toggle.
- **Install as an app.** Lightframes can be installed from the browser and works offline once loaded.
- **Try it without your own photos.** Sample photos to explore the editor, and a confirmation before clearing the workspace.
- **Better phone layout.** A cleaner toolbar and sidebar on small screens.

## 17 October 2026

- **Color and Effects panels.** Colour grading, vignette, grain, sharpening and noise reduction.
- **More local adjustments.** More mask types for local edits, and a white balance picker.
- **Export options.** Size presets (including Instagram sizes), quality, file naming and a text watermark.
- **Filmstrip filters.** Filter the photos in the filmstrip, with photo counts.
- **Crop with aspect ratios,** HSL colour mixer with hue, saturation and luminance per colour, and grouped sliders.
- **Touch support.** Pinch to zoom and pan, long-press to select in the filmstrip, and an overflow menu in the toolbar.
- **Editing workflow.** Undo/redo, before/after compare, crop / straighten / rotate, tone curve, histogram with clipping overlay, multi-select and sync edits, keyboard shortcuts, and your session restored after a page refresh.
- **Wider RAW support,** including Sony ARW files.
- **Presets:** HSL in presets, preset intensity, and `.cube` LUT import.

## 16 October 2026

- **First version.** A local photo editor in the browser: non-destructive sliders with a live preview, presets saved as JSON, copy/paste edits between photos, and JPEG export.
