## What this watermark is for

The watermark in Lightframes is one line of text on the file you download. Use it when a set should leave with the same signature: a delivery, a blog series, product photos, or a post. It is free, it needs no account, and it runs in the browser.

The longer guide, including a preset and an Instagram size in the same export, is [batch watermark photos](/blog/batch-watermark-photos). Copying the look across the set is the [batch photo editor](/tools/batch-photo-editor).

## How the mark is drawn

The mark is not a control in the edit panels. It lives only in the export dialog, and it stays off until you tick **Add**. You will not see it on the photo while you edit. It is drawn at the end of the export: after that photo's edits, after the resize, after an optional AI upscale, and after an exact frame is letterboxed. The letters are sized on the final image, so an upscale does not enlarge them.

No uploads. The browser reads the files from your disk and encodes the copy on your device. The original is not overwritten. The download is a newly encoded JPEG, PNG or WebP, so camera EXIF, GPS and the colour profile are not copied across.

The text is always **white**, in a **bold** sans-serif, with a **soft black shadow**. There is no colour control. A corner (the default is **bottom-right**) sits inset from the photo edge by **0.9 times the text height**. **Center** sits in the middle, with no inset. On an exact frame the photo is fitted and letterboxed, not cropped, and the mark is placed on that fitted photo, not in the empty border.

## Size and opacity

**Size** is the text height as a percentage of the **short edge** of the photo area, from **1% to 10%** in 0.1% steps. The default is **3.5%**. The drawn height is at least **10 pixels**. **Opacity** runs from **10% to 100%**, and the default is **70%**.

The field starts as `© `, so you can type a name after the copyright sign. The placeholder is `© Your name`. A long line does not wrap and can run past the edge, so keep it short, especially at the center or on a small frame.

Every file in that export gets the same line, the same corner, the same size and the same opacity. A different caption per photo means a separate export. **Copy edits** and **Paste edits** do not carry the watermark.

## What you can export

With the photos still selected, one set of options covers the whole selection:

- **Format:** JPEG, PNG or WebP. **Quality** is 50 to 100 for JPEG and WebP (default 92). PNG has no quality slider. A cut-out on a transparent background is saved as PNG even if JPEG was selected, and the watermark is still drawn.
- **Size:** Original (long edge up to 8192 px), Web (2048 px), IG 4:5 (1080 × 1350), IG 1:1 (1080 × 1080), Story (1080 × 1920), or a long edge from 800 to 8192 px. An exact frame also switches the format to JPEG. The border is white, black or a warm off-white, and the photo is fitted inside it.
- **Filename:** the pattern defaults to `{name}-edited`. JPEG uses a `.jpg` extension. `{name}` is that photo's name without its extension.

One photo downloads as that file. Two or more download as one ZIP, `lightframes-YYYY-MM-DD-N-photos.zip` (the date is UTC).

The [Instagram carousel maker](/tools/instagram-carousel-maker) is a different export. It does not draw this text watermark. Use the IG frames in the export dialog when you want the mark on single photos at those sizes.

## What it does not do

- **Text only.** One line, drawn once. No logo, no PNG stamp, no tiled pattern, no rotation, no second line and no colour other than white.
- **Not in the preview.** **Reset defaults** turns the watermark off and restores `© `, bottom-right, 70% opacity and 3.5% size, with the other export defaults (JPEG, quality 92, long edge 8192, filename `{name}-edited`). Those choices last for the session. A reload starts from the defaults again, with the watermark off.
- **One line for the selection.** Different text per photo is a separate export.
- **Metadata is not kept.** The file you opened stays as it was.

## Limitations

- If one photo fails to encode, the ZIP is not saved. Very large batches are limited by your device's memory, and the ZIP has to stay under 4 GB.
- Opening a folder needs a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave). Other browsers can still select many files with Open.
- The editor interface is in English. This page uses the labels you see on screen.
